## Project Overview

In this project, I explored Netflix's movies and TV shows catalogue to understand how its content is distributed across different genres, countries and release periods.

Using Python, I analysed 8,807 titles to identify patterns in content availability, compare movies and TV shows, and understand how Netflix's catalogue developed over time.

The aim was to turn raw data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis and visualisation.

## Dataset

The dataset contains **8,807 records and 12 columns**, including:

- Title and content type
- Director and cast
- Country of production
- Release year
- Date added to Netflix
- Content rating
- Duration and genre

**Dataset Source:** [Netflix Movies and TV Shows — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/netflix-shows)

## Project Objectives

1. Compare the distribution of movies and TV shows.
2. Identify the most common content genres.
3. Explore the countries contributing the most titles.
4. Examine trends in content additions over time.
5. Analyse movie durations and TV show seasons.
6. Interpret findings from a business analytics perspective.

## Key Findings

**1. Movies Dominate the Catalogue**

Netflix's dataset contains 6,131 movies (69.6%) and 2,676 TV shows (30.4%).

**2. Dramas Are the Most Common Primary Category**

Dramas account for approximately 1,600 titles when categorising content by its first-listed genre.

**3. The United States Leads Content Production**

The United States is the most frequently listed primary production country, highlighting its substantial representation in Netflix's catalogue.

**4. Content Additions Peaked in 2019**

The dataset records 2,016 titles added during 2019, the highest annual total in the analysis.

**5. Movie Duration**

The median movie duration is approximately 98 minutes, providing insight into the typical length of films in the catalogue.
